## Hames

When the Hames plugin is enabled, use `/ready` to review bounded work and `/go` in the same conversation to approve and execute the exact contract shown. Hames follows declared dependencies, applies proportional review, and writes durable decisions only to approved project locations. Use `/doctor` to diagnose configuration. Project-specific Hames context is in `.hames/`.
